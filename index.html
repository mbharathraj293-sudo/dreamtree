Our Story

The Dreaming Tree is an internationally influenced treehouse-style restaurant nestled in the holy town of Tiruvannamalai.

We pride ourselves on offering health-conscious cuisine, handcrafted organic beverages, and a peaceful sanctuary for travelers and locals alike. Immersed in lush green surroundings, our dining space is designed to align wellness, architecture, and gourmet tastes.

"Nourishment is not just food on a plate — it is the air, the shade of a tree, and the warmth of a shared moment."
