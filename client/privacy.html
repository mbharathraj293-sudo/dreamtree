Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

At The Dreaming Tree, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our guests' personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your details when you book a table, subscribe to our newsletters, or browse our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with us, we may collect the following details:

  • Contact details including your full name, email address, and telephone number.
  • Dining parameters such as date, time slot, guest counts, and special notes (allergies or preferences).
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, cookies, and usage telemetry.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal information to:

  • Confirm, reschedule, or cancel your table reservations.
  • Send you transactional SMS notifications and emails concerning your bookings.
  • Deliver newsletter digests, exclusive menus, and invites to botanical wellness events (with your consent).
  • Ensure site security and prevent fraudulent transactions or bot behaviors.

3. Security Measures

We store all records securely in our database. We employ advanced cryptographic protections (JWT authentication, database session validation, password hashing, and Helmet headers) to prevent unauthorized disclosures or leaks.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or removal of your personal information from our lists. If you wish to opt-out of emails, you can click the unsubscribe button in any message or contact our team directly.
